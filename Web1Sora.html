How to Play Sora

In MLBB, Sora (Shifting Cloud) is a strong fighter/assassin hybrid hero. He is played in the exp lane. His thunder form converts 30% of his health into damage, otherwise his torrent form converts his 1 attack into 10 health. His build is usually defense items and can depend on the enemy team composition.

Sora's Strengths

  • Teamfights
  • Securing Objectives
  • Split Pushing
  • Target Isolation
  • Escaping Danger
  • Vision Gathering

Sora's Abilities

  • Mystic Surge (Passive) - After using Sora's ultimate to ascend to Thunder or Torrent, 5 bars appear under his HP bar and can be filled up by dealing damage to enemy heroes or summoned units. Filling up these bars allows Sora to use his ultimate again dealing AoE damage.

  • Sundering Strike (Active) - Sora strikes twice and lunges forward dealing damage. After striking, Sora dashes to where the joystick is aimed.

  • Windstride (Active) - Sora Leaps forward dealing damage along the way. At max range he slams the ground dealing additional damage and slows down the enemy hero. Successful hits reduce the skill cooldown by 2 seconds.

  • Shifting Skies (Active) - Swipe left to ascend into Thunder for 10 seconds. Sora gains breif bonus Movement Speed and becomes untargetable. He the summons lightning to strike nearby enemies 3 times.
    Swipe right to ascend to Torrent for 10 seconds. After a brief delay, Sora pulls in nearby enemies, deals damage, and increases Sora's HP.
